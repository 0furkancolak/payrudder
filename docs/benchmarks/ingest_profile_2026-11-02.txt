Ingest at 20k and 10k events/s on the local compose stack, 2026-11-02 (plan 22 G17, docs/capacity.md). Nothing in the code was changed; experiments B to D change a setting of the scratch Postgres or ingest-svc's pool only.

Machine: Apple Silicon Mac, Docker VM with 2.9 GiB and 6 vCPU shared with ~20 containers of other projects; the host's load average was 6 to 15 (10 cores) during every run, mostly other people's builds and tests. Stack: reduced (Postgres 17, Redpanda, Redis, RabbitMQ, MinIO, Mailpit, plans-svc, ingest-svc, aggregator, limits-svc, billing-svc, assets-svc, gateway; no ClickHouse, analytics-svc, payments-worker, observability), SaaS mode off, one replica each, k6 2.3.0 (grafana/k6) on the same network and in the same VM. Binaries built on the host (GOOS=linux GOARCH=arm64) and packaged on distroless: building the images in the VM crashed Docker's engine once.

pg_test_fsync in the throwaway Postgres container, idle (load 7.8): fdatasync 126 ms, fsync 66 ms, open_sync 55 ms per 8 kB write; two 8 kB writes: fdatasync 32 ms, fsync 25 ms. (Normal for a local SSD: well under 1 ms.)
