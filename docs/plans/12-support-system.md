# 12 — Support system, finished

**Status (2026-11-02, second round): the operator's side is now a desk.** Built on top of the first round below: the operators are mailed when the person answers them, an unread badge for the person (the help link) and a waiting badge for the operators (the inbox link), canned replies, tags on tickets, an assignee chosen from the operators, a report of the queue and of the first-answer times, and a gauge with an alert (`SupportFirstAnswerOverdue`) on tickets waiting past their deadline. **Attachments** came in the third round (below). **Security review 2026-11-02 ([#375](https://github.com/0furkancolak/payrudder/pull/375)): the person's history shows only status and priority (an allow-list); the operator's tags and assignee are not in it, and the ticket's `assignee` is empty in the person's calls.** Still open: email-to-ticket, real-time updates, help for the seller's own customers (scope b). **Fourth round (branch `feat/leftovers-4-support`, section at the end): SLA by plan, a holiday calendar per app, merging two tickets of one person, a rating after a ticket is solved, search over the words of the messages, and links to a customer, an invoice or a subscription.**

**Status (2026-11-02, third round, branch `feat/product-tail-3`): attachments.** png, jpeg, pdf and plain text, 5 MiB each, 10 per ticket, 50 MiB per organisation, on the person's own messages and on the operator's (notes included). The bytes are in assets-svc (new purpose `support_attachment`, hidden from `ListAssets` unless asked for by name and not nameable through the tenant API; migration assets-svc `00002` with its tenant twin) under the **live tenant of the account's oldest app**; plans-svc keeps the record (`support_attachments`, migration `00022`, insert and select only) and the rules (who may attach to which message, the limits under the ticket's lock, no file on a closed ticket, a person never gets a file of an internal note). The gateway moves the bytes in three steps (authorize, upload, record; a file whose record fails is deleted again) and a download is a presigned link from assets-svc for that tenant. The console shows the files in the thread, a picker on the new-ticket sheet and both reply forms, and names a file that could not be attached while the message stays. **How it answers the reasons item 8 below gave:** (c) an operator path into another tenant's assets exists because the gateway presents the tenant the help desk names (assets-svc trusts the tenant of the call, as for every service); (b) the files sit with a live tenant, so a sandbox reset does not touch them (an app that is archived keeps its tenant); (a) the files **do count against that tenant's storage quota** (a full tenant refuses them with `support.attachment.storage_full`) and an account with no app cannot attach (`support.attachment.no_app`): these two stay as defaults (not agreed); (d) **no scanning**: assets-svc sniffs the type and checks the size, nothing reads the content, and a file stays as long as its ticket. **Not verified:** an upload through a real assets-svc and S3 from the gateway end to end (assets-svc's integration test and the gateway's fakes cover the two halves), the screens at phone width, Arabic by eye.

## Leftovers built (2026-11-02, branch `feat/product-leftovers-2-p12`)

Defaults the implementer chose (**default, not agreed**):

1. **Mail to the operators when the person answers.** One mail to every operator address (`ticket_customer_reply`, six languages, in the language of the operator's user), but only when the ticket was last answered by an operator: a second message in a row adds none (the ticket is in the queue and in their sidebar badge already). Why not every message: a person writing four lines in a row would send four mails. Links only; no ticket text in the mail (as for the others).
2. **Badges.** The person's: tickets they opened with an answer or a status change they have not read (`CountMySupportUnread`). The operators': tickets waiting for them (status open), from the report. Both refresh every minute and after a change; an unreachable count shows nothing.
3. **Canned replies** are shared by all operators (no per-operator ones), plain text, no placeholders, no categories; a change is everybody's and shows who saved it last.
4. **Tags** are the operator's labels: at most 5 per ticket, lower case letters, digits and hyphens, at most 30 characters; never shown to the person; filterable; in the history. No tag list or colours.
5. **Assignee** is an operator address from `PAYRUDDER_OPERATOR_EMAILS` (the one list the gateway lets into the console and the mails go to). A ticket keeps an assignee that is not on the list (one typed by hand before) until it is changed. **Not built:** operator roles, "my tickets", automatic assignment.
6. **Report:** counts of the queue and the first answers of the **last 30 days** (wall clock, not business hours: the deadline is in business hours, the time people feel is not), median and average, answered in time. Not built: resolution time, per-operator figures, a chart, a period choice.
7. **SLA alert:** gauge `support_sla_breached_tickets{priority}` (and `support_waiting_tickets`) from plans-svc's own database; `SupportFirstAnswerOverdue` is a **warn** after 10 minutes with any ticket late (not a page: an urgent ticket that is late is a person's business, not a night call; raise it if the owner wants). A rule test (`promtool test rules`) holds it firing and resolving.
8. **Attachments: not built in the second round, and why (answered in the third round: see its status).** assets-svc keys every file by tenant and by a purpose list in its proto (`invoice_logo`, invoice attachments, general files), and counts it against that tenant's storage quota. A ticket belongs to an **account**, so the review's default (store under the account's first tenant with a ticket purpose) would (a) spend the seller's own storage quota on evidence for support, (b) lose the evidence when that tenant is deleted or its sandbox reset, (c) need an operator path into another tenant's assets, which assets-svc does not have (every read is for the tenant of the credential), and (d) leave the plan's "attachment scanning" unanswered (assets-svc checks size and type, not content). A safe version needs an account-level purpose and quota in assets-svc, an operator read, and a scanner; that is its own plan, not a leftover. Until then a ticket takes text only, and the person pastes a link.

Tests: plans-svc integration `TestPlansSvc/SupportDeskExtras` (the operator mail once until the operator answers again, the unread count and its clearing, tags normalised / in the history / filtered / **never in what the person sees**, the assignee rules, the report as differences, the gauge read through the OpenTelemetry SDK with a 0 for a priority with none, canned replies and their validation, the operator-only calls refused), `Support` and `SupportDesk` (assignee now an operator address), unit tests of tags and assignee (`domain`), the six-language mail texts (`usermail`), gateway `TestSupportDeskRoutes` and the OpenAPI route check, `promtool test rules`, console unit tests (`duration`, the operator paths) and the mock e2e `support.spec.ts` (tags, canned replies, the report with the sidebar badge, the help link's badge). **Not verified here:** the mail in a real mailbox and its wording by a native speaker; the badges and the report against the real API in a browser; the gauge on a running Prometheus (the rule is checked with promtool); the screens at phone width and in Arabic (right to left).


## Leftovers built, round 4 (2026-11-02, branch `feat/leftovers-4-support`)

Defaults the implementer chose (**default, not agreed**; the owner may overturn them). Details in [plans-svc](../03-services/plans-svc.md#as-built-the-help-desk-plan-12).

1. **SLA by plan.** The first-answer deadline follows the ticket's priority and the **plan of the ticket's app** (`tenants.tier` of its live tenant): free (every new app, and the table the desk always had) urgent 1 h, high 4 h, normal 9 h, low 27 h; standard 1 / 2 / 6 / 18 h; enterprise 30 min / 1 / 4 / 9 h, in business hours. A ticket about the account as a whole takes the best plan among its apps; an account with no app is free. The table is in code (`domain.SupportFirstResponse`), not editable by the operator: the numbers are a proposal, the shape is the decision. The plan is kept on the ticket and shown to the operator only.
2. **Holiday calendar.** A list of dates **per app**, kept by the **operator** (not by the customer: the days off are those of the people who answer; a customer who could add them could only slow their own answer). The business-hours clock skips them like a weekend. Stored as calendar days in the app's time zone; at most 200 within three years. Opening a ticket counts with the days as they are; adding days later does not move a deadline already set (a change of priority counts it again). Not built: a calendar of public holidays by country that fills itself, a default calendar for apps with none.
3. **Merge.** Operator action on two tickets of **one person in one account**; messages and files move to the kept ticket, the other is closed with a pointer and takes no more writing. Not built: merging tickets of two people, undoing a merge, merging by the person.
4. **Satisfaction.** 1 to 5 and an optional comment, by any member of the account, on a solved or closed ticket; a second rating replaces the first; the operator's report shows the count, the average and the spread of the last 30 days, and the ticket shows the rating and the comment. Not built: a mail asking for it, a rating per answer, a trend chart.
5. **Search.** Postgres full text over the messages (`simple` configuration, whole words, `websearch_to_tsquery` syntax): the operator's inbox over everything, notes included; the person's list over the public messages of their own accounts. No prefix search ("webh" does not find "webhook"), no ranking (the list stays newest first).
6. **Links.** A customer (external id), an invoice or a subscription (UUID) of an app environment of the account, at most five per ticket; given when the ticket is opened and replaced by the operator. Only the pointer is stored and nothing checks that the object exists; the console draws it as a link to the page of the record under the viewer's own access.

Not built, and why: **email-to-ticket** and **real-time updates** (the plan lists inbound mail and a push channel as undecided infrastructure: plan 11, and a channel decision), a table the operator edits for the SLA, mails for a merge or a rating request (no new mail was needed; the six-language templates are untouched).

Tests that fail without the change: plans-svc integration `TestPlansSvc/SupportLeftovers` (the deadline for each plan, hidden from the person; days off stop the clock and a priority change counts again; merge: who may, the refusals, the thread in one place, both histories, no more writing; the rating and the report as differences; search: the person never finds a word of an internal note, the operator does, another account's tickets are never found, odd input never fails; links: two kinds in two environments, the refusals, nothing half-made; three of these were switched off once (the internal-note filter, the days off, the same-person rule) and the subtests failed), `testSupport`/`SupportDesk` follow the new signatures, unit tests in `domain` (the table, days off, rating, link, holiday and merge rules) and `support` (the allow-list gains `merge`), gateway `TestSupportLeftoversRoutes` and the OpenAPI route check, console mock e2e `support.spec.ts` (seven new tests) and the report test. **Not verified here:** the screens at phone width and in Arabic (right to left) by eye, the new strings by a native speaker, the links against a real invoice page, the merge with real attachments through assets-svc (the files' rows move; the bytes do not), and the search on a large table (the GIN index is there, no load was run).
