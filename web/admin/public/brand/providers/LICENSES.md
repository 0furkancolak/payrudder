# Provider marks

Only licensed or official marks are shown in the console's Integrations page. A provider without one is shown by its plain text name; nothing here is drawn
or imitated by hand. Each file below records where it came from and under which terms.

The mark itself remains the trademark of Paddle.com Market Limited and is shown only to name the payment provider the customer connects (nominative use). The only change is the fill: `#111111` for the light theme and `#FFFFFF` for the dark theme. ## Shown by name only

None at the moment: every provider with a brand has a licensed or official mark.

## Generic icons (not brand marks)

Bank transfer, the built-in simulator and the custom (webhook) e-invoice integrator use general-purpose icons from `@lucide/vue` (ISC licence), because
they name a method and not a company.
