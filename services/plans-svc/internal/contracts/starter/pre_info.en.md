## 4. Right of withdrawal

Send your statement to: [seller: e-mail address or postal address]. You may use the model withdrawal form of the Regulation or any clear statement.

## 5. Where there is no right of withdrawal
Services performed instantly in electronic form, and services whose performance starts with your approval before the withdrawal period ends, are exceptions to the right of withdrawal (Turkey: Regulation on Distance Contracts, article 15(1)(ğ) and (h); EU: Directive 2011/83/EU, article 16(a) and (m)).
